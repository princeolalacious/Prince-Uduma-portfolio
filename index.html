    About

    Who I am, how I work, and what I can build for you.

    I'm Prince Uduma, from Lagos, NIgeria. A Frontend Developer passionate about engineering high-performance, responsive, and visually engaging web experiences. I specialize in translating UI/UX designs, product concepts, and business requirements into pixel-perfect, accessible, and scalable web applications.

    My core stack includes HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), Tailwind CSS, and React.js. I build component-driven interfaces, develop reusable UI components, implement responsive layouts, integrate REST APIs, and focus on clean, maintainable code, cross-browser compatibility, and performance optimization. Beyond custom frontend development, I also leverage WordPress and WooCommerce to deliver dynamic, content-managed websites and e-commerce solutions.

    I approach development with both an engineering and product mindset. From understanding user requirements and translating design systems into functional interfaces to optimizing Core Web Vitals and improving user experience, I focus on building solutions that are technically sound, intuitive, and aligned with business objectives.

    I'm continuously expanding my technical capabilities in Next.js, TypeScript, and backend development, with a growing interest in full-stack engineering and modern web technologies.

    I'm open to freelance engagements, remote opportunities, technical collaborations, and early-stage startup projects. I bring a problem-solving mindset, attention to detail, and the commitment to deliver reliable solutions from concept to deployment.
